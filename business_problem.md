# Business Problem — Retail Customer Behavior & Sales Intelligence

## Business context
A retail business wants to understand how customers purchase, what product categories drive revenue, how discounts and subscriptions relate to customer behavior, and which customer groups deserve targeted retention or marketing attention.

## Objectives
- Quantify revenue and order performance.
- Identify high-revenue categories and products.
- Compare subscriber and non-subscriber behavior.
- Segment customers by previous purchase history.
- Evaluate discount usage and product-level discount dependency.
- Compare shipping methods and payment methods.
- Build an executive-friendly Power BI dashboard.

## Key questions
1. Which categories generate the most revenue?
2. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
3. How does subscription status relate to spending?
4. Which products have high discount rates?
5. Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
6. Which products receive strong customer ratings?
7. How do shipping and payment methods relate to order value?

## Scope and limitations
This dataset contains 3,900 customer purchase records and no transaction date. Therefore, the project focuses on customer/product/category behavior rather than true time-series sales forecasting.
